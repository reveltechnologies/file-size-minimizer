# File Size Minimizer

Shrink videos, photos, PDFs, and text/code files to fit upload limits, strip hidden metadata, and view local files on your phone or browser. Everything runs on your device: **your files never leave your device.**

---

## How to use

1. **Select a file** (or drop/paste a file, or pick multiple photos to combine into one A4 PDF).
2. **Choose a target size**: pick a preset (`Discord`, `WhatsApp`, `Email`, `ID Photo`, `Form`, `Portal`, `Document`, `100 KB`–`1 MB`) or enter a custom KB/MB limit, and check the live **Estimated size**.
3. **Open More options** if needed (keep original 100% size or resize, adjust quality/FPS/sound, change photo format to `JPG`, `WebP`, or `PNG`, toggle black & white, or switch text files between minification and `.gz`) and tap **Shrink**.
4. **Save, share, or open in View**.

---

## Presets

| File type | Preset | Limit |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Video | Discord | 10 MB |
| Video | WhatsApp | 16 MB |
| Video | Email | 25 MB |
| Photo | ID Photo | 500 KB |
| Photo | Form | 1 MB |
| PDF | Portal | 2 MB |
| PDF | Document | 5 MB |
| PDF | Email | 10 MB |
| Text | 100 KB / 500 KB / 1 MB | 100 KB / 500 KB / 1 MB |

Sizes use decimal units (1 MB = 1,000,000 bytes) so the output fits strict upload forms everywhere.

---

## Options

| Option | Description |
| --- | --- |
| **Remove hidden info** | Strips EXIF, GPS location, camera serials, author tags, and PDF revision history (enabled by default). |
| **Resize / Resolution / Image size** | Keep **Original (100%)** dimensions or scale down to fit tighter size limits. |
| **Quality & Format** | Choose **JPG**, **WebP**, or **PNG** (when transparent) and pick **Low**, **Medium**, **High**, **Original**, or **Auto** quality; optional **Black & white** for photos. |
| **Frame rate & Sound** (video) | Adjust FPS (**Original**, **30 fps**, **24 fps**) and audio (**Normal**, **Low**, **Off**). |
| **Method** (text/code) | **Keep format** (strip comments, blank lines, and extra indentation where supported) or **Compress (.gz)**. |
