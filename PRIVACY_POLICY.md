# Privacy Policy

_Last updated: November 8, 2026_

**File Size Minimizer** ("the App") is developed by [Revel Technologies](https://github.com/reveltechnologies).

## 1. No Data Collection

The App does not collect, transmit, store, or share any personal information, files, photos, videos, documents, device identifiers, or usage analytics.

## 2. 100% On-Device Processing

All file compression, format conversion, metadata removal, and file viewing run locally on your device. Your files never leave your phone or computer.

## 3. Local Storage & History

- **Settings**: Your language, theme, output preferences, and default options are stored locally on your device (`localStorage`).
- **Recent Files**: When enabled in Settings, the last 10 viewed and last 10 shrunk files are stored locally on your device (`IndexedDB`) so you can open or save them again. You can clear or disable this history at any time in **Settings**, or by uninstalling the App.

## 4. Permissions

The App uses your device's system file picker to open only the specific files you select. It does not request background access to your photos, media library, camera, microphone, contacts, or location.

## 5. Third-Party Services

The App contains no advertising SDKs, analytics trackers, crash reporters, or external cloud APIs.
